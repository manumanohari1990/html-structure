Facts about Octopus:

An octopus has three hearts. Two hearts pump blood to the gills, while the third pumps blood to the rest of the body. Interestingly, an octopus has blue blood!

How Intelligent Are Octopuses?

Octopuses are highly intelligent sea creatures. They can solve puzzles, recognize people, open jars, and even use tools to protect themselves.

Fact 2: Lightning Is Extremely Powerful!

Lightning is a giant spark of electricity that occurs during thunderstorms. A lightning bolt can reach the ground or travel between clouds.

How Hot Is Lightning?

A lightning bolt can heat the surrounding air to approximately 30,000 Kelvin (about 27,700°C), making it much hotter than the surface of the Sun.

Repeated Lightning Strikes

Lightning can strike the same place multiple times. Tall buildings and towers are often struck repeatedly during thunderstorms.

Fact 3: The Deep Ocean Is Still Unexplored!

More than 80% of the world's ocean is still unmapped, unobserved, and unexplored in detail. Scientists continue to discover new marine species and underwater environments.

How Deep Is the Ocean?

The deepest known point in the ocean is Challenger Deep in the Mariana Trench, which is nearly 11,000 meters below sea level.

A Fun Comparison!

If Mount Everest were placed inside the Mariana Trench, its peak would still be more than a mile underwater!
