Stay Healthy with Exercise!

Exercise is very important for children. It helps us build strong muscles, healthy bones, and a strong heart.

Playing outdoor games, cycling, dancing, and swimming are fun ways to stay active.

Remember: Children should get at least 60 minutes of physical activity every day!

Quick Health Tips

  • Eat fresh fruits and vegetables every day.
  • Drink plenty of water.
  • Exercise and play outdoor games.
  • Brush your teeth twice a day.
  • Wash your hands before eating.
  • Get enough sleep every night.
